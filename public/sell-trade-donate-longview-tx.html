Sell, trade or donate in Longview

The counter runs three ways in: straight buys, trades, and donations that go straight onto the thrift racks.

Call (903) 704-9393 Directions & contact

Selling to the shop

Jensing's buys items — furniture and clothing are what fill the shelves, so those start the conversation fastest. Bring the piece by during open hours, or call (903) 704-9393 before a big drop-off so the counter can tell you whether there's room and whether it fits what the shop sells.

Trading

Money isn't the only way in: the shop takes trades. An item for an item works here — ask at the counter about what's short on the shelves that week.

Donating

Donations are the thrift side. What people leave at the door gets priced 'super, super cheap' and sold to the neighborhood — the same donation-fed model behind the Gifts of Grace thrift sales owner Jennifer Beddingfield used to organize for a Longview nonprofit. One family's cleared-out closet becomes someone else's $6 find.

And there's room growing for more: the shops' shared storage area is planned as off-home garage-sale space — ask at the counter where that stands.

Frequent questions

Do you buy furniture and clothing?

Yes — the shop buys items and accepts trades, with furniture and clothing as its focus.

How do I sell something?

Stop by Suite B during open hours or call first — especially before a big load.

Can I trade instead of sell?

Yes. Trades are welcome, not just straight buys.

What happens to my donation?

It goes on the thrift side at 'super, super cheap' prices and goes out the door again to the next household.

Something to clear out?

Call (903) 704-9393 or bring it by Tue–Sat, 10–7 — 813 E Methvin St Suite B.
