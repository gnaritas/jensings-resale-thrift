From the Gifts of Grace shelves to Methvin Street

Before this shop, owner Jennifer Beddingfield spent years organizing the Gifts of Grace thrift sales that benefited a Longview nonprofit. Jensing is her nickname from her music-ministry work — and this shop is the next chapter, a mother-daughter operation under one roof with her daughter Jasey's vintage store, Ill x Intent Vintage.
