One storefront, two sides

The resale side is the curated side — items the shop has bought and picked over time. The thrift side is the donated side — pieces people bring or drop off, priced what the owner calls 'super, super cheap.' Both sides carry the same kinds of things: used furniture and clothing.

The shop's own yardstick for vintage is simple: 20 years or older. Anything that old that makes it onto the shelves is vintage by definition.

The storefront is shared — Jensing's runs under one roof at 813 E Methvin St Suite B with Ill x Intent Vintage, the vintage comics-and-media store run by Jennifer Beddingfield's daughter Jasey. One building, two shops, two very different treasure hunts.

A shared storage area behind the shops is planned as off-home garage-sale space — ask at the counter where that stands on the day you visit.
