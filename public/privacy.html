Privacy

Plain terms, because this is a thrift store, not an ad network. Last updated November 13, 2026.

What the contact form collects

When you send a message from this site, you give us your name, and optionally a phone number and an email address, plus whatever you write in the message. That goes to the inbox that runs this site, so the shop can answer you. It is used for that reply and nothing else — no mailing lists, no selling or sharing of your details.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no tracking or analytics scripts. There is nothing following you from page to page.

The embedded map

The directions map on the home and contact pages is served by Google. When it loads, your browser talks to Google's servers, and Google may apply its own privacy policies and cookies for that map. The map doesn't know who you are beyond that request.
