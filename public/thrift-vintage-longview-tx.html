The resale shelves

The resale side is the bought-and-chosen side: pieces the shop has picked up and curated over time. These are the keeps — furniture and clothing that made the cut on quality and character, priced like the finds they are.

The thrift racks

The thrift side runs on donations. People bring or drop off what they're clearing out, and the shop prices it what the owner calls 'super, super cheap' — furniture you can actually fit in a car and clothing that doesn't make you think twice.

What 'vintage' means here

The shop keeps one rule: 20 years or older is vintage. If a piece clears that bar, it's vintage — no pedigree required, just years and use. Because the thrift side is stocked by whatever the neighborhood donates, the racks change with every round of drop-offs.

And because the storefront is shared with Ill x Intent Vintage next door in the same Suite B, a Tuesday can cover two shops: furniture and clothing here, comics, records and VHS one step away.

Frequent questions

What do you carry?

Furniture and clothing — curated resale on one side, donated thrift on the other.

20 years or older. That's the shop's definition.
